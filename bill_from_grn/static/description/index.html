Bill From GRNs - Purchase Receipt Billing

This module streamlines the vendor billing process by enabling direct bill creation from Goods Receipt Notes (GRNs). Say goodbye to manual bill entry and ensure accurate billing based on actual received quantities!

Create Vendor Bills in Two Flexible Ways:

  • From Purchase Order: Select one or multiple GRNs linked to a purchase order and generate bills instantly
  • From GRN Directly: Create bills directly from the receipt/picking form without navigating to purchase orders

Key Features:

  • Direct GRN-Bill Linkage: Bills are automatically linked to their corresponding receipt documents for complete traceability
  • Multi-GRN Billing: Combine multiple receipts into a single consolidated vendor bill
  • Automatic Data Population: Received quantities and prices are automatically populated in the bill
  • Flexible Workflow: Choose the approach that fits your business process best
  • 3-Way Matching Support: Perfect for organizations implementing Purchase Order - Receipt - Bill matching

Benefits:

  • ✓ Reduces manual data entry and eliminates errors
  • ✓ Improves billing accuracy based on actual received quantities
  • ✓ Accelerates the accounts payable process
  • ✓ Provides better tracking and audit trail
  • ✓ Supports partial billing scenarios for partial deliveries
  • ✓ Saves time for accounting and purchasing teams

Option 1: Create Bill from Purchase Order

Navigate to Purchase → Purchase Orders → Select your order

Click the "Create Bill from GRN" button to open the wizard

Select GRNs and Create Bill

Select one or multiple GRNs (receipts) associated with the purchase order

The system will automatically create a bill with the received quantities

Option 2: Create Bill Directly from GRN

Navigate to Inventory → Operations → Receipts (or Transfers)

Open a validated receipt and click the "Create Bill" button

Bill Creation

The bill is automatically created and linked to the GRN after click on create button

All received quantities and prices are populated automatically

Direct Linkage Between GRN and Bill

View linked GRNs directly from the vendor bill

Complete traceability from purchase order → receipt → bill

View Linked Bills from GRN

From any receipt, you can easily view all bills created from that GRN

Perfect for tracking partial billing scenarios

Multiple GRNs in One Bill

Combine multiple receipts into a single consolidated vendor bill

Ideal for suppliers with multiple partial deliveries

Use Cases

  • Manufacturing Companies: Receiving materials in multiple shipments and creating bills per receipt
  • Distributors: Managing partial deliveries from suppliers with accurate billing
  • Service Organizations: Implementing strict 3-way matching (PO-Receipt-Bill) for compliance
  • Retail Businesses: Processing bills based on actual received inventory
  • Any Business: Requiring consolidated billing for multiple receipts to reduce payment processing
